## en-US
**Short description (80자 이내)**
Point, show, done. Korean phrase cards for taxis, food, shopping & more.

**Full description**
Traveling in Korea? Skip the awkward miming. Show Korean turns what you need to say into big, clear Korean cards you can simply show to staff.

• 10 everyday situations: Taxi, Subway & bus, Restaurant, Café, Convenience store, Shopping, Pharmacy, Hotel, Directions, Emergency
• 88 ready-to-use sentences — "Not spicy, please", "I'm allergic to peanuts", "Can I get a tax refund?", "Where do I transfer?"
• Taxi destinations: tap a famous place or paste your hotel's Korean address
• Two-way: staff tap a reply ("Card is OK", "Sold out", "Over there") and it appears in your language
• 🔊 Listen to the Korean pronunciation
• Save your favorite phrases
• Works offline — no data needed
• No sign-up, no account
• English, 日本語, 繁體中文

Emergency numbers are built in: Police 112, Fire & Ambulance 119, Korea Travel Hotline 1330 (24h, multilingual).

• 🔊 韓国語の読み上げ
• お気に入り保存
• オフラインで使える・登録不要
